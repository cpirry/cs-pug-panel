Drop map thumbnail images in here to have them show up on the Maps tiles
in the panel.

Naming: <map name>.jpg (or .png / .webp), matching the map's internal name
exactly, e.g.:

  de_dust2.jpg
  de_inferno.png
  de_ancient_night.webp

No rebuild or restart needed - the panel serves this folder directly.
Maps without a matching image just show a plain placeholder tile.
